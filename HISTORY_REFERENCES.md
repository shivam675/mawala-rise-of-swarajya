# Historical references and boundaries

Research checked 21 October 2026. This is an early playable interpretation requiring further historical and Marathi-language review.

## Sources

- [Maharashtra Tourism, Forts pocket guide](https://maharashtratourism.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Maharashtra-Booklet_compressed.pdf): government tourism material identifies Torna in Pune district and describes Shivaji Maharaj's capture in 1646. Search excerpts were available; the full PDF exceeded the reading tool's size limit. Used only for broad setting/date context, not exact architectural dimensions.
- [Maharashtra State Gazetteers, Pune District](https://gazetteers.maharashtra.gov.in/Pune%20District/Pune%20District.pdf): indexed history chronology lists Torna, 1646. The full PDF could not be fetched reliably. No detailed claim is attributed to unread pages.
- [Maharashtra Ahead, August 2015, Directorate General of Information and Public Relations](https://dgipr.maharashtra.gov.in/sites/default/files/2020-08/MAhead-AUG%202015.pdf): government publication discusses the early Hindavi Swarajya context around 1645–46 and Torna as an early acquisition. Used as background, without reproducing quotations.
- [Hill Fort of Torna, digitized historical material, Gokhale Institute repository](https://dspace.gipe.ac.in/xmlui/bitstream/handle/10973/29544/GIPE-194427.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3): search excerpt describes Torna/Prachandgad and a traditional 1646 dating. Older accounts are context, not definitive evidence for fictional mission details.

## What the game asserts

The region is inspired by Maval and Sahyadri hill-fort landscapes in Maharashtra around 1646. Torna and early Swarajya provide broad historical context. Labels say “circa 1646” and “inspired by.” The game does not present the royal coronation, later capital, later naval campaigns, or battles of later decades as events of this opening.

## What is fictional or unverified

Ambe village in this game, the young player warrior, Badal, Keshav, Jiva, Suryaji, Gauri, Bapu, the ridge patrol, all dialogue, supplies, quest order and the “First Oath” are invented training-story elements. Their names do not assert identity with documented historical individuals. The quest is **not** a reenactment of a documented oath ceremony or the capture of Torna.

Terrain, fort footprint, gateways, keep, courtyard, water cistern and routes are designed for traversal. They are not surveyed reconstructions. The fort's game label denotes inspiration and should not be used for tourism/navigation. Saffron banners and the turban/tunic/shield/sword silhouette are broad visual interpretations; exact dyes, cuts, weapon profiles and rank distinctions have not been independently authenticated.

Shivaji Maharaj is not given invented dialogue or represented as a player-controlled character. No fictional conversation or reference-image slogan is described as a historical quotation.

The Devanagari title “मावळा” and English subtitle structure are included. Draft Marathi dialogue needs review by a fluent Marathi editor before it is exposed as a complete localized experience; current gameplay subtitles are English.
